Delete your Savify account

Option 1 — in the app (instant)

1. Open Savify and sign in. 2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. 3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account and ALL your data (transactions, bills, loans, budgets, goals and balances) are permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2 — by email

If you can't open the app, email savify.help@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with and write “Delete my account”. We delete everything within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is kept?

Nothing. We don't keep copies of your financial data after deletion. Purchase records may be kept by Apple or Google as required by law.
