Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Savify ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal and financial information when you use the Savify mobile application. By using Savify, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address and display name through our authentication provider (Supabase Auth). Financial Data: We store the financial information you voluntarily enter, including income and expense transactions, loan records and payment history, savings goals and progress, budget goals, and recurring expenses. App Preferences: Settings such as your preferred currency, language, and display theme are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to: • Provide, operate, and maintain the Savify service • Sync your financial data securely across your devices • Display personalised financial summaries, reports, and insights • Send in-app notifications for recurring expenses or reminders (only when enabled by you) • Improve the performance and stability of the app We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the Savify service to you.

4. Data Storage

Your financial data is stored in Supabase, a cloud database platform that uses industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS in transit. Supabase infrastructure is hosted on AWS and is SOC 2 Type II certified. Your data is stored in a dedicated database partition and is logically isolated — only your account can access your records. For details on Supabase's data practices, see: supabase.com/privacy

5. Authentication

Account creation and login are handled by Supabase Auth, part of the same secure platform that stores your data. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed by Supabase. Session tokens are stored on your device and expire automatically. See: supabase.com/privacy

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal or financial data with any third parties for marketing, advertising, or commercial purposes. Third-party services that process data: • Supabase — login and database storage (see Sections 4 and 5) • Google Gemini — ONLY when you choose to scan a receipt, the photo is sent to Google to read the amount and shop name. The photo is not stored by Savify. Google may use free-tier requests to improve its services, so do not scan documents with sensitive personal details. • Apple / RevenueCat — only to process Pro subscriptions Smart Coach tips are calculated on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

7. Security

We take the security of your financial data seriously. Measures we implement include: • Encrypted data transmission (TLS/HTTPS) between your device and our servers • Encrypted storage at rest in our database • Logical data isolation — each account can only access its own records • Token-based authentication with session expiry No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong password and keep your device secure.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to: • Access: View all your financial data within the app at any time • Correction: Edit or update any data you have entered • Deletion: Permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Delete Account. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone • Portability: Request an export of your data by contacting us at savify.help@gmail.com If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you may also have rights under GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

9. Children's Privacy

Savify is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or via the email associated with your account. Continued use of Savify after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us: Email: savify.help@gmail.com We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 2 business days.
