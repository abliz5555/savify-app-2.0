Savify helps you track spending, bills, loans and savings — simple, private and free.

Need help?

Email us at savify.help@gmail.com. We reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

I didn't get the verification code. Wait a minute and check your spam folder, then tap “Resend code”.

I forgot my password. On the Sign In screen tap “Forgot password?” and enter the code we email you.

How do I back up my data? Settings → Data → “Save a backup”. Restore it any time with “Restore from backup”.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Account → Delete Account. See Delete account.
